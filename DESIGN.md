# Founders & Pitchers — design direction

Revision: the website should feel like a Toronto event someone actually organizes, rather than a luxury-community template. The page's job is to make the evening understandable and make the next event easy to find.

Palette: paper #F3F0E8, ink #111111, night #0B0B0B, vermilion #E33B2E, accessible red #B62D23, rule #CEC9BE. Keep the established palette; change its use rather than invent another brand.

Type: Manrope 750–800 for the main display, Manrope 450–650 for reading and interface copy, upright Instrument Serif only for editorial quotations. No decorative italics. Larger body copy, fewer uppercase labels, and fewer forced two-line slogans.

Layout alternatives:

A. Old split: [serif headline / italic ending] [photo + decorative stamp]. Rejected: the repeated typography and decorative badges make every section feel interchangeable.
B. New programme: [large plainspoken headline 8 columns] [context + actions 4 columns], followed by [wide candid image 9 columns] [next session 3 columns]. Selected: the next event becomes part of the opening composition, and the people have room to be visible.

Signature: a red event notice attached to a wide photographic strip, followed by an interactive three-part evening schedule. Dates and times encode actual event information; there are no arbitrary coordinates, asterisks, fake barcodes or decorative quotes.

Critique: big sans-serif type alone would still feel generic. Ground the opening in leaving work for a Toronto workshop, give the schedule useful interaction, retain sample labels, and replace repeated claims about “better rooms” with practical information. Keep the archive readable and make the membership section describe what members get. No invented social proof.

Motion: direct tab selection, a brief photo/content fade, existing responsive navigation and accordion transitions. No autoplay, scroll hijacking or ornamental motion. Respect reduced motion.
