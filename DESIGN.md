# Founders & Pitchers — design direction

A Toronto evening translated into paper: an intimate documentary photograph, monumental type, and a vermilion admission ticket. The singular signature is the physical ticket; circles, badges, and decoration stay subordinate.

Tokens: paper #F3F0E8; ink #111111; night #0B0B0B; vermilion #E33B2E; accessible red #B62D23; rule #CEC9BE. Instrument Serif for expressive editorial headlines, Manrope for navigation and compact, confident information. A twelve-column grid, 1440px maximum, with independently composed mobile layouts.

Hero: [editorial headline 7 columns] [documentary photograph 5 columns], a small admission slip bridging the image and caption. The next event becomes a full-width vermilion ticket. Alternate generous cream compositions with a near-black manifesto, restrained archive tickets, and an intimate monochrome contact sheet.

Critique before building: the cream/serif palette is explicitly requested, but could default to a generic lifestyle site. Ground it in a specific Toronto night: date-led event ticket, neighbourhood captions, practical lesson titles, editorial rules, and purposeful sans-serif interludes. Avoid fake attendance, invented testimonials, and corporate headshots. Demo events and stock atmosphere are labeled on the visible site.
